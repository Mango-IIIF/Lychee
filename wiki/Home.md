# Mango Annotate documentation

Mango Annotate is a IIIF annotation editor built on the
[Mango IIIF viewer](https://github.com/Mango-IIIF/Mango). It opens a IIIF
Presentation manifest, lets people draw and describe annotations on its
canvases, and exports them as a W3C Web Annotation `AnnotationPage`.

## Start here

- [Getting started](Getting-Started) — add the editor to a page or an app.
- [Events](Events) — listen for edits and exports, and save them.
- [W3C and IIIF support](Annotation-Support) — what the editor reads, writes,
  and does not round-trip.

Viewing, theming, plugins and the rest of the viewer are documented in the
[Mango viewer wiki](https://github.com/Mango-IIIF/Mango/wiki).
